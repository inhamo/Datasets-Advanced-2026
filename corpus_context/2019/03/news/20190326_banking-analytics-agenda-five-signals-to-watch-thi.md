Key banking signals for March 2019:

- Loan payment volume: 450 records in comparable internal files.
- Failed-payment rate: 7.8%, with insufficient funds still the main pressure point.
- Processing risk: 10 timeout-related rows point to operational follow-up.
- Digital adoption: industry data indicates channel growth of about 18.0% year on year, driven by app, wallet and USSD activity.
- Customer growth: benchmark estimates show an increase in customers of roughly 3.4% year on year across comparable retail portfolios.
- Control focus: finance teams are separating normal bank charges and interest from genuine reconciliation breaks.

Implication:
Banks need a single management view that connects customer growth, product usage, repayment stress, payment delays and statement exceptions.
